Emotional Stability Index

Measures how consistent and calm your emotional tone is across speech. A higher score means your voice shows steady, neutral-positive affect — a sign of low emotional stress. Sudden tone swings or heightened emotional volatility lower this score.

Speech Fluency Index

Tracks the smoothness and continuity of your speech. It penalizes frequent pauses, filler words ("um", "uh", "like"), incomplete sentences, and mid-thought restarts. A high score means you spoke with confidence and flow — an indicator of healthy verbal processing speed.
